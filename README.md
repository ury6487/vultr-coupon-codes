The other restrictions, in order of how often they surprise people:

- **Full rates resume after the credit burns down.** Treat the promo as temporary runway. Once it's gone, you pay normal hourly/monthly rates for whatever you're still running, so size your deployment with that day in mind rather than discovering it on an invoice.

## FAQ

**Can I use more than one Vultr coupon code?**
No. The official terms say promotional credit cannot be combined with any other offers. Pick the single offer that fits your plan and apply that one.

**Do Vultr promotional credits expire?**
Yes. The FLY trial codes expire 30 days after signup, and the promotional half of a VULTRMATCH deposit match runs 12 months. Confirm the exact dates in your Billing page after activation, since that's where the real deadline lives.

**Why isn't my Vultr coupon code working?**
The most common causes are an expired promotion, a product that's excluded from promotional credit, or an account that doesn't qualify as a new customer. Codes that circulate on third-party sites often no longer appear on Vultr's own coupons page — check there first.

**Is a Vultr coupon the same thing as managed hosting?**
No. Vultr is unmanaged IaaS — you get root access and you administer the OS yourself. Managed WordPress that happens to run on Vultr's infrastructure (for example, through Cloudways Flexible, which sits on Vultr as a separate managed layer) is a different product with different billing, and coupon codes for one don't apply to the other.

**Who qualifies for the current codes?**
New customers only, per the listing on Vultr's coupons page. Existing accounts and duplicate accounts are typically excluded, and the offers can't be combined with each other.
